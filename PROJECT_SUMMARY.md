## 🎯 Features Overview

### 🚫 Logical Fallacies (8 Types)
1. **Ad Hominem** – Attacking person, not argument
2. **Appeal to Authority** – "Expert said it"
3. **Hasty Generalization** – One example = all
4. **False Cause (Post Hoc)** – Timing = causation
5. **False Dilemma** – Only 2 options exist
6. **Circular Reasoning** – Conclusion in premise
7. **Straw Man** – Misrepresenting opponent
8. **Appeal to Emotion** – Feelings over logic

### 📊 Evidence Analysis
- Count of evidence claims (0-5+)
- Quality rating (None → Substantial)
- Visual strength bar (0-100%)

### ⚔️ Counter-Arguments
- 2-3 opposing viewpoints generated
- Helps prepare for debate
- Shows potential weaknesses

### 🔍 Underlying Assumptions
- 2-3 hidden assumptions revealed
- Shows what argument depends on
- Questions the logic

### 💡 Recommendations
- 4 specific, actionable improvements
- Prioritized by impact
- Tells exactly what to fix

### 📈 Overall Assessment
- Strength badge (Strong/Moderate/Weak/Very Weak)
- Personalized feedback text
- Clear next steps
